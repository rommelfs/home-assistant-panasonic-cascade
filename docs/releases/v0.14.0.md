# 0.14.0 — Heat-pump electricity sources

Experimental prerelease.

Add read-only, explicitly estimated PV/grid/battery attribution for aggregate heat-pump electrical consumption. Existing PV measurement/sign mappings are reused, with optional independent metering configuration. Accounting runs independently of PV-charge permission and at night; stored battery energy is not relabelled as PV.

Expose current source power, persistent cumulative kWh totals and local-calendar day/week/month/year counters. The Energy view adds absolute source values, current power history and hourly/daily/weekly/monthly/yearly charts, retaining existing cards. Values begin at setup; earlier periods cannot be reconstructed reliably. Invalid/stale/contradictory feedback and downtime create gaps rather than invented zeros or backfilled energy.

This is a documented PV-first household balance followed by proportional attribution to all heat pumps, not direct source metering. Includes unit, conservation, night/battery, restart, calendar/DST, dashboard-preservation and real HA lifecycle tests. The feature does not modify heating or battery controls.
