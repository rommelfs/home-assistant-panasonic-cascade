# v0.18.1 — Electricity-source shares at a glance

The overview consumption card now lists grid, PV and battery contributions in kWh and percent for today. Percentages use the attributed energy as their denominator, not the full WP daily counter. Partial recording and observation time remain visible, alongside the signed difference against WP counter statistics. A positive difference is unassigned consumption; a negative difference can result from delayed counter statistics.

Unknown source readings retain a dash rather than becoming zero. Percentages are unavailable if any source is missing or the attributed total is zero. Attribution and heat-pump control logic are unchanged.

Validated with native Home Assistant template rendering for unequal source shares, partial-day recording, missing readings and zero totals, plus the full integration regression suite.
