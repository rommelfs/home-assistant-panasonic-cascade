# 0.13.0 — Continuous automatic PV charging

Experimental prerelease.

Healthy automatic charges are renewed five minutes before the current block expires, normally adding another 30 minutes. Renewal persists the new deadline without restoring/reapplying the controller offset. The automation no longer stops merely because a healthy 30-minute block has elapsed.

New configurable defaults: 30-minute minimum charge-request hold, 120-second low-positive-margin delay, and a persisted ten-minute cooldown before another automatic start, followed by a fresh stable-surplus interval. A brief low-margin dip at a block boundary gets the remaining debounce time instead of an immediate stop or another full block.

Temperature, measurement freshness/validity, grid-import, battery-discharge, exhausted battery-reserve and controller guards still take priority. No new permission to draw battery/grid power is introduced. Manual stop, restart recovery and compare-before-restore ownership remain effective; storage failures during renewal request restoration. Explicit manual actions and sensorless tests retain their fixed deadlines.

The minimum hold concerns the integration's heating request, not a guaranteed compressor runtime. Panasonic retains compressor control. Dashboard status distinguishes renewable automatic blocks and exposes hold/cooldown timers. Protocol and HA lifecycle tests use simulated hardware; field verification of the new policy remains outstanding.
