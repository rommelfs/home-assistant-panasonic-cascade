The new HAR captures extend the integration with equipment and energy details and an alarm view.

- Per heat pump: detail-view power, working hours, the controller's displayed error code, and additional raw data points.
- Real kWh counters: only energy points explicitly marked as totalized receive Home Assistant energy statistics. W/kW remain power. The dashboard adds an energy view with daily statistics once Home Assistant has recorded sufficient data.
- Equipment and plant configuration exposed as read-only diagnostics: model names/codes, rated capacity, zones/sensor types, tank connections, PID/cascade settings, and setpoint offsets. These additions do not create new write controls.
- Alarms: current count, number of records in the past year, the latest alarm, and up to 20 displayed entries with time, equipment, text, and status. Current and historical alarms remain separate. No acknowledgement or deletion actions are added.
- The domestic-hot-water option also hides newly discovered domestic-hot-water power/energy points, unit setpoints, and connection diagnostics.
- Temperature sentinel −32768 is treated as unavailable. Other unusual readings remain unchanged.

Current supplementary values share one `evalAll` request. Equipment parameters and alarm history refresh only every five minutes. Failures of individual optional queries do not stop normal point polling.
