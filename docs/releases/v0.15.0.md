# 0.15.0 — Adaptive PV buffer temperature stages

Experimental prerelease. Explicit opt-in, automatic mode only; not enabled for existing installations by updating.

Adaptive PV charging starts at the lowest affordable temperature stage and can gradually raise the buffer target through 45, 50, 55, 60 and 65 °C. Stage budgets are respectively 3, 4.5, 6, 8 and 10 kW after the battery reserve, including existing heat-pump electrical consumption. The latter prevents the controller from withdrawing a stage just because its own demand reduces export. The budget is also capped to measured PV minus the configured battery reserve in priority mode. Unknown or stale aggregate WP power blocks adaptive charging.

Upward steps require the next stage's budget plus 500 W, stable for five minutes. Downward steps use a 750 W deadband and two-minute confirmation. Temperature-stage changes are spaced at least ten minutes apart. Lower temperatures are deliberately preferred; this is a surplus policy, not a measured COP optimiser, forecast, guaranteed compressor runtime or exact electrical-power controller. Existing immediate safety/measurement guards still win, and automatic charge blocks can renew without an off/on interruption.

This opt-in buffer-only path exceeds the advertised 0–10-degree metadata range. On the reference P-SMART EDGE installation, a brief authorized probe confirmed offsets 12/16/20/25/31 degrees yielding buffer targets 46/50/54/59/65 °C with zone demand unchanged at 34 °C. That verifies target processing on this installation, not manufacturer approval or a sustained tank charge. Adaptive offsets are therefore bounded to the tested 31-degree maximum and targets to 65 °C. When zone demand is below 34 °C, 65 °C may not be reachable within this offset envelope; the policy uses the highest eligible lower stage. A fresh, compatible metadata profile and actual temperature feedback are required. Different/tighter metadata profiles are refused.

Every elevated write verifies the buffer target and zone demand. The original default and pending retarget are persisted before changing the controller, so a lost reply or restart restores either the old or pending applied offset. Independent changes are preserved. A changing zone demand is compensated to keep the absolute PV target; an invalid/overshooting target, unexpected feedback or a zone outside the verified envelope ends the boost. No heating curve, zone enable, SG Ready, device safety limit or heating-mode selector is edited.

Manual charges, sensorless tests and the ordinary permanent-offset number retain the 10-degree maximum. Adaptive mode requires a real temperature source and a stop ceiling from 40 to 65 °C. The ceiling is a polled software guard on the selected probe, not a hardware cutoff or a guarantee against thermal overshoot.

Status attributes and generated dashboards expose the adaptive budget, active target and candidate stage. Existing dashboard cards and source-energy accounting are retained. Unit/protocol and real Home Assistant lifecycle tests cover stage timing, hysteresis, source failures, the tested write envelope, target verification, pending-write recovery, reload and restoration.
