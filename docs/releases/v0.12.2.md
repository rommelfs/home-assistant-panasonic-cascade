# 0.12.2 — Live PV charging status

Experimental prerelease.

Refresh PV charge status attributes during an active charge, including native temperature report age/count, current surplus, effective buffer offset, targets and remaining duration. The charging branch previously validated guards but returned without notifying the status entities, leaving the dashboard at its start-of-charge snapshot.

The controller lease, original-offset restoration, 1,000 W battery reserve and temperature safeguards are unchanged. Includes the genuine repeated ESPHome packet handling introduced in 0.12.1.
